## Keywords and titles: no search manipulation
Source: https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/listing-policies/search-browse-manipulation-policy?id=4243
Every word in the title, description and keywords must be true of the item being sold.
Adding popular search terms that don't describe the item, to get more views, is not allowed.
Do not compare the item with other products, and do not use a listing to promote other
listings. Keywords ending in a question mark are not allowed; if a detail is unknown, leave
it out rather than guessing. One listing sells one product: no mixing brands, models or
conditions (for example new and used) in the same listing.

## Brand names: "for" and "compatible with"
Source: https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/listing-policies/search-browse-manipulation-policy?id=4243
For an accessory that fits another product, put "for", "fits" or "compatible with" before
the other product's name, e.g. "Clear Case for iPhone 16 Pro". This wording is NOT allowed
in front of brand names for clothing, jewelry or accessories, or for universally compatible
items: a dress cannot be listed as "for Zara" or "Reformation style". Only name a clothing
brand if the item is genuinely made by that brand.

## Brand names: trademarks and VeRO
Source: https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/listing-policies/selling-policies/intellectual-property-vero-program?id=4349
Brand names and logos are trademarks. Only use a brand name if the item is made by that brand
or is specifically compatible with it. A generic phone case must not be listed under the
brand "Apple"; naming a different brand to divert search traffic is not allowed. Slightly
altered brand names, third-party logos in the description, and phrases that imply the seller
is affiliated with or endorsed by a brand are not allowed. Rights owners can have listings
removed through eBay's VeRO program.

## Counterfeits and "dupes"
Source: https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/prohibited-restricted-items/counterfeit-item-policy?id=4276
Counterfeit, replica and unauthorized copies are prohibited, including items that use a
brand's name, logo or protected design to look authorized. The word "dupe" combined with a
brand name is not allowed because it suggests a counterfeit. Listing language must not make
false claims about materials, origin or safety certifications.

## Write your own text and photos
Source: https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/listing-policies/images-text-policy?id=4240
Sellers should write their own descriptions and take their own photos. Copying text or images
from other websites, other sellers, catalogs, adverts or product packaging is not allowed
without the rights owner's written permission. Factual specifications that can't be phrased
another way, such as measurements or weight, are fine to state. Content must be truthful
and accurate.

## No contact details or off-eBay links
Source: https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/listing-policies/links-policy?id=4248
Listings must not send buyers off eBay to buy, and must not include phone numbers, emails or
external website links (including in images or video). Links are only allowed to other eBay
pages, approved product video sites and legally required information, and must use HTTPS.
Store names can't be web addresses.

## No unverifiable reputation claims
Source: https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/listing-policies/thirdparty-endorsements-policy?id=4249
Do not include third-party statements, badges or logos vouching for the seller's
trustworthiness, or claims about the seller's reputation that buyers can't verify on eBay.

## One listing per item, variations in one listing
Source: https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/listing-policies/duplicate-listings-policy?id=4255
A seller may not run several fixed-price listings of the identical item at the same time.
Sizes and colors of the same dress belong in one listing with variations. Accessories that
fit several devices may have up to 5 separate listings if each names the device it fits.

## Offensive content
Source: https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/prohibited-restricted-items/offensive-materials-policy?id=4324
Listings and items that promote or glorify hatred, violence or discrimination are not
allowed, including slurs, demeaning caricatures, and hate symbols such as Nazi or Confederate
battle flag imagery on modern products (for example a printed phone case or dress).

## Product safety and recalls
Source: https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/prohibited-restricted-items/product-safety-policy?id=4300
Items that are recalled, banned or don't meet safety regulations can't be listed. Check the
US CPSC recall list. Children's products carry extra rules, and products with easily reachable
button batteries are not allowed. Removing recalled listings promptly is also a User
Agreement obligation.

## Prohibited and restricted items overview
Source: https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/prohibited-restricted-items/prohibited-restricted-items?id=4207
eBay keeps per-category rules for items that are banned or need conditions met, including
weapons and accessories, drugs and paraphernalia, adult items, alcohol and tobacco, hazardous
materials, animal products, medical devices, counterfeit goods, offensive materials, personal
information, recalled products and items that encourage illegal activity. Ordinary women's
dresses and phone cases are allowed as long as they are genuine and safe.

## User Agreement: seller is responsible for AI-written listings
Source: https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/member-behaviour-policies/user-agreement?id=4259
Under the User Agreement (Listing Conditions), the seller is fully responsible for the item
and for the accuracy of the listing, explicitly including text produced with translation or
generative AI tools. eBay may edit or remove content that breaks its policies, and search
placement depends partly on policy compliance and relevance. Recalled or unsafe products must
be removed promptly.

## User Agreement: content license and accuracy
Source: https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/member-behaviour-policies/user-agreement?id=4259
By listing, the seller grants eBay a broad license to use the content and promises that it is
accurate, legal and doesn't infringe anyone's intellectual property. eBay's own catalog
product data may be used only in eBay listings. The eBay name and logos may not be used
without permission.

## Selling policies overview
Source: https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/selling-policies/selling-policies?id=4214
eBay's selling policies cover selling practices, seller performance standards, fees, taxes,
intellectual property (VeRO) and prohibited items. Breaking them can lead to listings being
removed and selling limits or account suspension.
