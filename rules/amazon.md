## Title length and item highlights
Source: https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/GYTR6SYGFA5E3EQC?locale=en-US
Titles for non-media products must be 75 characters or fewer, including spaces. Extra detail
that doesn't fit, such as materials or use cases, goes in the separate Item highlights field
(up to 125 characters, written as short comma-separated phrases, not sentences).

## Title characters, repetition and promotion
Source: https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/GYTR6SYGFA5E3EQC?locale=en-US
Titles may not contain ! $ ? _ { } ^ ¬ ¦. Symbols like ~ # < > * are only allowed in a real
context such as "Style #4301" or "<10 lb", never as decoration. No word, including the brand,
may appear more than twice (small words like "and", "for", "the" are exempt). No promotional
phrases such as "free shipping" or "best seller", and no restricted phrases such as
"FSA/HSA eligible".

## Title style and word order
Source: https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/GYTR6SYGFA5E3EQC?locale=en-US
Recommended order: brand, style, product type, key attribute, color, size or pack count,
model number, e.g. "Brand Women's Wrap Midi Dress, Floral, Medium". Capitalize the first
letter of each word except short prepositions, conjunctions and articles; never ALL CAPS.
Use numerals ("2-Pack") and standard abbreviations for measurements ("in", "oz"). No
subjective claims like "Hot Item". For variations, size and color go in the child listing
title, not the parent's. Phone accessories should name the exact compatible model.

## What is not allowed anywhere on the detail page
Source: https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G200390640?locale=en-US
Titles, bullets, descriptions and images must not contain: obscene or offensive content;
phone numbers, addresses, emails or URLs; price, availability or condition details;
shipping offers such as free shipping; reviews, quotes, testimonials or requests for reviews;
adverts or watermarks; time-sensitive information; another brand's intellectual property on
a generic product; HTML or other code (line breaks in the description are the only
exception).

## Capitalization and emojis in all fields
Source: https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G200390640?locale=en-US
Titles, bullet points and descriptions should capitalize only the first letter of each word
and must not be written in all capitals. Emojis, emoticons or symbols at the start of words
are prohibited.

## Accuracy and claims
Source: https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G200390640?locale=en-US
Product information must be accurate and not mislead customers about the product's qualities.
Claims are limited by the Prohibited product claims policy (for example unproven health,
antimicrobial or "FDA approved" claims). Mention any extra items essential for the product
to work. Deliberately inaccurate or miscategorized listings can lead to losing selling
privileges.

## One product, one detail page
Source: https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G200390640?locale=en-US
Don't create a new detail page for a product already in the catalog. A new version (a change
in color, size, material or features) needs its own page rather than editing an old one.
Products sold under a different brand need their own ASIN; never change the brand on an
existing listing. Detail pages can't be used to cross-promote other products.

## Intellectual property and brand use
Source: https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G200390640?locale=en-US
Listings must not infringe other parties' intellectual property. A generic product must not
carry a brand's name or logo as if made by that brand; mention another brand only to state
compatibility (for example "for iPhone 16"). Product IDs such as UPCs must be genuine and
owned or authorized (GS1).

## Restricted products overview
Source: https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G200164330?locale=en-US
Products must be legal, safe and allowed by Amazon. Restricted areas include alcohol,
weapons and explosives, drugs and paraphernalia, hazardous items, medical devices, cosmetics,
supplements, offensive and controversial materials, recalled products, surveillance and lock
picking equipment, and prohibited product claims. Some categories need Amazon's approval
first. Children's apparel and children's footwear have their own restricted-product rules,
so girls' dresses need extra care; ordinary women's dresses and phone cases are allowed.

## Business Solutions Agreement: accurate product information
Source: https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G1791?locale=en-US
The seller must give accurate, complete required product information and keep it up to date.
Products, packaging and listings must follow all laws, including age, marking and labeling
rules, and must not contain defamatory, obscene or (unless a policy allows it) sexually
explicit material. Excluded products can't be offered, and web addresses can't be used as
brand marks. Products needing a California Proposition 65 warning must provide it.

## Business Solutions Agreement: data and AI restrictions
Source: https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G1791?locale=en-US
Sellers may not use Amazon's materials or services to build or improve AI or machine-learning
models, or use robots or data-mining tools to collect Amazon content.

## Business Solutions Agreement: enforcement
Source: https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G1791?locale=en-US
Amazon can withhold payments, suspend or terminate an account for policy breaches, deceptive
activity or selling counterfeit goods, and offers an appeal process. The agreement includes
binding individual arbitration.
